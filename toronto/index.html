What a Toronto booking includes

The wedding packages do not change because the city does. Essential is up to 6 hours and 200+ photos, Signature is up to 8 hours and 300+ photos, and Premium is up to 10 hours and 400+ photos. Signature and Premium include a complimentary engagement session. You still get the private gallery, printing rights, and no watermarks.

Toronto adds one line: the travel add-on is $299. That is the same fee listed for Montreal. It sits on top of the package, so an Essential wedding in Toronto starts at $2,699, Signature at $3,298, and Premium at $4,798.

Tell me every address for the day when you write. Hotel, ceremony, and reception in different neighbourhoods is normal, and I plan the hours around those moves. Extra time beyond the package is $250 an hour. A second photographer is quoted separately if the guest list needs one.

Hours, locations, delivery

Signature is the package most couples choose: eight hours covers getting ready, the ceremony, portraits, and well into the reception. Premium is the one for a long Toronto day with more than one location and no gap in the middle. Essential fits a ceremony-focused booking when the celebration is shorter.

A 50% retainer holds the date. The rest is due two weeks before the wedding. I will give you the delivery window when we confirm the booking, based on the size of the day, and you will not be waiting months.
