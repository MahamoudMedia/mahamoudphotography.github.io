About Me

I'm Mahamoud, a documentary and editorial photographer based in Ottawa. Most weekends you'll find me at a wedding here, in Toronto, or in Montreal.

What started as a high school project turned into four years of professional work built entirely on word-of-mouth. My approach is photojournalistic at its core: I work the room, document the day as it unfolds, and stay out of the way. When it's time for portraits, I shift into a loose editorial style: intentional framing, good light, images that feel composed without feeling staged.

My Somali-Canadian roots taught me what a real celebration looks like — loud rooms, full tables, the kind of energy you can't manufacture. I don't create moments. I just make sure none of them get missed.
